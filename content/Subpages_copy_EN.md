> # From brain drain to brain circulation

### 01 · Point of departure

Human capital accounts for roughly two-thirds of global wealth. The share grows with income level: from about 40% in low-income countries to around 70% in high-income ones.¹

Ukraine approaches this reality under compounding pressure — demographic loss, large-scale wartime displacement, and intensifying global competition for skilled professionals. The resource most critical to its future is currently contracting fastest.

Migration policy that merely responds to outflows cannot address this. What is required is a systemic approach that treats mobility as a structural given and channels it into circulation: the return of knowledge, experience and connections to the economy, even when people themselves remain abroad.

Launching and sustaining that circulation is the purpose of the 3R Model.

> **Where the model comes from.** 3R builds on two decades of research into transnational entrepreneurial networks — most directly AnnaLee Saxenian's account of how the circulation of engineers between Silicon Valley and Taiwan, India, China and Israel converted brain drain into mutual advantage. The model accepts that conclusion — circulation is possible and beneficial — but addresses the question that work leaves open: whether circulation can be deliberately initiated where its organic preconditions (peacetime growth, decades of network maturation, functioning capital markets) are absent. **3R is a framework for engineered circulation under compressed timelines and adverse conditions.**

¹ *World Bank, The Changing Wealth of Nations 2018.*

### 02 · The unit of analysis

**The circulation transaction** — an interaction in which a carrier of human capital and an economic actor jointly produce a result: knowledge applied, a decision implemented, a connection that leads to collaboration.

A contact does not constitute a transaction. A registration does not constitute a transaction. Circulation means these interactions recur or persist over time.

A transaction is **compounding** when it generates derivative transactions in which the original intermediary no longer participates — a mentee who becomes a mentor, a company that goes on to engage further experts on its own, a connection that produces new connections. The share of transactions that compound is an early, measurable signal of the long-term effect this model exists to produce.

---

### 03 · The wartime modality

Classic accounts of brain circulation assume physical mobility: professionals commuting between locations, building trust in person on both ends. Wartime conditions break this assumption directly — martial-law mobility restrictions, security risk, insurance and logistics, the inaccessibility of parts of the territory.

The model therefore distinguishes two classes of transactions:

**Remote-compatible** — knowledge transfer, advisory and consulting roles, mentorship, remote embedding into teams. Fully available under wartime constraints.

**Presence-dependent** — physical return, on-site expert missions, permanent resettlement. Constrained by the security situation and the mobility regime.

This distinction is not a limitation — it defines the deployment sequence. Circulation deliberately begins with remote-compatible transactions, builds trust and institutional infrastructure through them, and expands into presence-dependent forms as conditions allow.

> **The circulation of knowledge does not wait for the free movement of people.**

---

### 04 · Return — restoring connection

*(The three R sections share one layout: definition sentence set large, then body.)*

**Return is the capacity to rebuild trust and reactivate human capital that is temporarily outside the domestic system.**

It encompasses the return of citizens, the integration of the diaspora's accumulated expertise, capital and institutional know-how, the circulation of knowledge, and the restoration of professional networks.

Return operates through different modes of participation: permanent presence; dual engagement — where a person remains abroad while staying economically active in Ukraine; and regular circulation between locations. The goal is not demographic return as such but the restoration of **economic inclusion**: the capacity to generate value within the economy, regardless of the mode of presence.

Return requires an institutional carrier. Dual engagement does not sustain itself through events and newsletters — it requires a permanent receiving structure inside the country: one that matches returning capital to demand, accompanies transactions through to outcome, and remains the stable counterpart across repeated engagements. International experience is consistent: diaspora circulation scaled where such standing structures existed, and stalled where engagement remained episodic. Within the Next Ukraine ecosystem, that receiving function is UGTI.

---

### 05 · Recruit — structural reinforcement

**Recruit is the capacity to close structural competency gaps by attracting professionals with no prior connection to Ukraine.**

Unlike Return, which reactivates existing capital, Recruit introduces new competencies — those the domestic labour market cannot supply on its own at a given stage of development. It does not add headcount mechanically; it makes targeted adjustments to the configuration of human capital where internal resources are insufficient.

Integration works best through employers with genuine demand — direct channels between carriers of scarce competencies and the business environment, rather than public programmes alone.

**Recruit works only where the value proposition is explicit.** A professional with no prior connection to Ukraine engages for identifiable, named reasons:

- **The scale and uniqueness of reconstruction tasks** — professional challenges that exist nowhere else; rare career capital.
- **Sector growth windows** — rapidly developing sectors offer roles and responsibility unavailable in saturated markets.
- **A long-term institutional horizon** — the EU accession trajectory anchors expectations of convergence and stability.
- **Remote-compatible entry** — the first engagement requires no relocation, lowering the threshold.

Recruitment instruments that fail to communicate this end up competing on compensation alone — a competition a wartime economy cannot win.

---

### 06 · Retain — an environment for application and accumulation

**Retain is the central element of the model: the capacity to create conditions in which human capital is applied, integrated, networked, and compounds over time.**

Retain targets **internal brain waste** — the condition in which available competencies go underused. Its measurable indicator is the over-qualification rate: the share of people employed below their qualification level.

Retain operates through three levers:

**Lever 1 — Reskilling.** Redirects idle or misaligned capital toward application. It works on three levels at once: expanding the surface area for skills application, protecting capital from depreciation as demand shifts, and generating new professional networks. Reskilling is effective only when **demand-coupled** — tied to a specific employer need or project. Reskilling without demand reproduces brain waste at a higher level. The relevant measure is not programme completion but employment in the new qualification and its retention over time. Delivered through circulation — diaspora members and external experts as mentors — Retain connects back to Return: repatriated experience becomes an instrument for strengthening domestic capital.

**Lever 2 — Access to capital.** Skills without capital do not compound. The comparative evidence is unambiguous: where circulation was accompanied by investment infrastructure, returning and recruited talent founded and scaled enterprises; where it was not, circulation stalled at supplying skilled labour to foreign demand. *This is a theoretical lever of the model, not a current mandate of Next Ukraine. As UGTI builds a validated pipeline of teams and projects, financing instruments may become the subject of a future-phase decision — not part of the current plan.*

**Lever 3 — Career trajectory.** Retention is a function of the visible future, not only the present position. Capital stays where a credible internal trajectory of growth exists — advancement, responsibility and recognition attainable without leaving the country. Where the ceiling is visible and low, retention fails regardless of current conditions. Retain treats the legibility of career trajectories in strategic sectors as a design object, not a by-product.

### 07 · The compounding effect

*(Formula set large, navy on paper — the visual centre of the page.)*

> **3R × time → compounding of human capital**

The model's central hypothesis: when all three components operate simultaneously and systematically, a multiplier emerges — knowledge reinforces knowledge, networks grow denser, productivity increases non-linearly.

**This is a hypothesis, not an established fact.** The compounding effect unfolds over years and cannot be confirmed or refuted by short-term data. Early signals, however, are observable sooner:

- transactions grow faster than the number of participants;
- the same individuals return and engage repeatedly;
- competency gaps in key sectors narrow;
- a growing share of transactions generates derivative transactions without the original intermediary.

**The absence of these signals over a three-year horizon is grounds for reviewing the mechanisms.** If the hypothesis holds, human capital transitions from a resource to a strategic asset.

---

### 08 · Two waves of diaspora

Ukraine's diaspora is not one population but two, and the model engages them through different channels.

| | Historical diaspora (post-WWII) | Wartime wave (post-2022) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary resource** | Capital, institutions, established networks, political access | Current professional knowledge, active ties, motivation, language |
| **Primary channel** | Transactions: investment, institutional partnership, sponsorship of circulation infrastructure | Knowledge circulation and dual engagement: advisory, mentorship, remote embedding, eventual return |
| **Dominant transaction class** | Presence-independent capital transactions | Remote-compatible knowledge transactions, converting to presence-dependent forms over time |

There are also substantial flows whose skills require realignment with the future economy: returnees, veterans, holders of structurally redundant competencies. For these groups reskilling is not an option but a mechanism of integration.

### 09 · Actor architecture

The model operates through four levels of actors, each with a distinct role and its own measures of success. *(Four horizontal bands.)*

**The state — policy and systemic conditions.** Establishes the regulatory environment, incentives and programmes that make circulation possible. Indicators: changes in conditions — legislation, access, incentives.

**Intermediary organizations — activation and conversion.** Organize the diaspora resource into a functioning network, build trust where the state has not yet re-established it, create channels for transactions. In conditions of institutional weakness the intermediary temporarily carries the trust function the state cannot yet provide — not a stopgap, but an architectural feature of the reconstruction period. *Within the Next Ukraine ecosystem, this level is UGTI.* Indicators: volume and quality of transactions, repeat-participation rate, conversion of connections into transactions.

**Business and employers — demand and absorption.** The level at which circulation is absorbed into the economy. Employers articulate genuine demand — the starting point of every demand-coupled transaction; they integrate professionals into real tasks; they co-finance circulation where its value is demonstrable. Without this level the other three produce connections with nowhere to land. Indicators: articulated competency demands, share of transactions completed to an applied outcome, repeat engagement, employer co-financing as a share of transaction cost.

**The diaspora — resource and partner.** Carries capital, networks and motivation, but may be unorganized. Activation proceeds through three sequential channels: **information** (learning of opportunities) → **transaction** (a specific request with a concrete outcome) → **network** (members connect through shared activity). Information without transactions does not produce circulation. Transactions without connection do not form networks. The network is the carrier of the long-term compounding effect.

---

### 10 · Measurement logic

The model is manageable only when each actor distinguishes the indicators they are responsible for from those they merely influence.

**Three levels of indicators**

- **Context** — national human-capital stock: supply, outflow rate, over-qualification. Establishes the baseline; not a measure of any implementer's success.
- **Contribution** — what an actor directly produces: connections, circulation transactions, application of new qualifications. The primary KPIs at each level.
- **Impact** — long-term effects (productivity, network density, compounding) to which an actor contributes but does not claim as its own.

**The transaction ladder — measuring trust**

Trust is the scarcest resource of circulation and the hardest to observe directly. The model measures it behaviourally:

**Step 1 — First successful integration.** Conversion from contact to completed integration measures the credibility of the offer.
**Step 2 — Repeat participation.** The repeat rate measures satisfaction and the durability of the relationship.
**Step 3 — Referral.** A participant brings in a new participant, vouching with their own reputation. People recommend only what they trust — and networks grow through referral faster and more cheaply than through outreach. **A rising share of new participants arriving through referral is the single strongest early indicator that the trust infrastructure of circulation is forming.**

**Three rules**

1. Measure integrations and outcomes, not registrations and reach.
2. Measure application, not completion.
3. Distinguish contribution from causation.

This discipline is a condition of the model's credibility — and the foundation of a methodology transferable to public policy.

### 01 · The problem UGTI solves

Ukraine has one of Europe's largest communities of professionals abroad — engineers, entrepreneurs, researchers and managers integrated into international innovation ecosystems, with access to modern technologies, management practice and global networks.

Yet the interaction between these networks and domestic economic processes remains fragmented. Contacts arise through personal connections, individual projects, short-term initiatives — without institutional structure. The result is a structural gap between the global human capital connected to Ukraine and the concrete tasks of developing its economy.

The most valuable competencies — management judgment, quality standards, market understanding — are not written down in textbooks. They live in people's experience and transfer only through real work on real tasks. Training and one-off consultations do not close this gap. **UGTI exists to close it institutionally.**

### 02 · Build · Integrate · Host

*(Three numbered bands, echoing Home 03 but with full depth.)*

**i · Build — UGTI's own instruments**

- **The Ukraine Competency Gap Atlas.** UGTI does not work with "talent" in the abstract. The Atlas captures the specific competency gaps that constrain a sector's development and can be closed through external expertise — built through interviews with sector leaders, anchored to the 3R sector criteria: knowledge density, multiplier effect on adjacent sectors, availability of diaspora expertise, strategic priority. The model is anchored to the criteria, not to a fixed list.
- **The Argonaut mechanism.** Sourcing and selection of the global professionals and entrepreneurs UGTI integrates into Ukrainian teams — with rigorous verification of experience, motivation and fit for the case.
- **The integration-support methodology.** The process discipline that turns a match into an outcome.

**ii · Integrate — expertise into real tasks**

Integration at UGTI is a managed operational process, not a matching service:

1. **Problem definition.** Partner and NUF jointly define the expected outcome, constraints and success criteria. Integration begins with research into the company's real needs — not with an offer.
2. **Sourcing and selection.** Experts drawn through global networks, assessed for expertise and for the ability to operate in the Ukrainian context.
3. **Embedding.** The critical step. NUF facilitates the format of cooperation, runs onboarding, defines the expert's role in the operating structure, sets up interaction with the team.
4. **Support to outcome.** Ongoing monitoring, format adjustments as the task evolves, NUF as facilitator and quality controller throughout.

The engagement format varies by task — short-term advisory to full embedding; remote or in Ukraine. Regardless of format, every integration runs with clear stages, metrics and accountability for the outcome.

> **The core difficulty of this model is not sourcing experts — it is integrating them effectively into local processes. That is NUF's core competency.**

**iii · Host — an institutional umbrella**

Valuable initiatives that advance Return, Recruit or Retain often emerge before they have institutional capacity — registration, governance, compliance, initial funding. Without an institutional partner they either never launch or spend their first years building administration instead of doing the work. Next Ukraine closes this gap in two formats:

- **Fiscal sponsorship** — for initiatives without their own registered entity. Next Ukraine receives donor funding on the initiative's behalf, carries compliance, reporting and legal support, and provides part of the operational infrastructure.
- **MOU** — for initiatives with their own registration. A lighter format defining scope, brand terms, coordination with 3R logic, and mutual reporting.

Selection criteria: the initiative clearly reinforces one or more components of 3R; it creates no mission conflict or reputational risk; its team has demonstrated delivery capacity; the affiliation benefits both sides.

### 07 · How a sector changes

When several companies achieve concrete results, that becomes a precedent for the industry. Demand for international expertise grows organically, network density increases — and the market gradually begins to function without ongoing UGTI intermediation. That is the design goal: **an autonomous market in which business sources experts independently.** UGTI measures its success by approaching obsolescence in each sector it enters, then moving to the next.

### 02 · Why an intermediary, and why now

Ukraine has strong actors on human capital — analysis (KSE, CES, CEDOS, OECD, IOM), policy (BRDO, ILO, the State Strategy for Demographic Development to 2040), and program delivery (veteran programs, Diia.Business, donor-funded reskilling). **That is the function NUF is designed to perform.** Not replacing state strategy — operating the mechanics underneath it. Where institutional trust is still being rebuilt, an intermediary temporarily carries the trust function the state cannot yet provide.
