### 01 · The case

> ## The resource most critical to Ukraine's future is the one contracting fastest

Human capital accounts for roughly two-thirds of national wealth. Ukraine is losing it to demographic decline, wartime displacement and global competition for skilled professionals — simultaneously. Migration policy that reacts to outflows cannot fix this. What is required is a system that treats mobility as a given and channels it into circulation.

**Figures** *(four tiles: one large, three supporting; source on hover)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **4.4M** | Ukrainians hold temporary protection in the EU, down from a peak of 5.7M. Protection runs to March 2027 — for many, the decision to return is deferred, not made. |
| **20–33%** | Of the economic value an average EU country generates per hour worked. Against Germany: 10–14%. |
| **74%** | Of Ukrainian employers named labour shortage a serious problem in 2025. Over 200,000 vacancies unfilled. |
| **2026–2040** | The window in which it is still possible to influence where competencies land and how dense professional networks become. Under martial law it is held open not by mass return, but by the circulation of knowledge. |

### 02 · The model

> ## Enabling brain circulation as a system, not a slogan

**Three cards**

**RETURN — Restoring connection**
Reactivating human capital that sits outside the domestic system — permanent presence, dual engagement, or regular circulation. The goal is economic inclusion, not demographic return.

**RECRUIT — Structural reinforcement**
Closing competency gaps with professionals who have no prior connection to Ukraine. Targeted, not mass — and only where the value proposition is explicit.

**RETAIN — An environment for accumulation**
The central element. Conditions in which capital is applied, networked and compounds: demand-coupled reskilling, access to capital, legible career trajectories.

**One-line band under the cards:**

> Circulation starts remote — the first transaction requires no relocation. **Knowledge moves before people can.**

`Read the full model — including what would prove it wrong →` /model

### 03 · What we do

> ## Three functions, one operating engine

Next Ukraine puts the model into practice through **UGTI — Ukraine Global Talent Integration**: connecting global expertise to the concrete modernization tasks of Ukrainian companies, institutions and regions.

**i · Build** — the Competency Gap Atlas, the Argonaut sourcing mechanism, the integration methodology.
**ii · Integrate** — external expertise embedded into real tasks, managed to a measurable outcome. The hard part is not finding experts — it is integration. That is the core competency.
**iii · Host** — an institutional umbrella for programs that advance 3R. UKRIC, a K–12 invention-education program, is the first.

**Evidence strip (one line, three figures, links to /ugti#evidence):**

> It works where it has been tried: **+179% revenue** (Modern Expo, Ukraine) · **3,500+ engineers integrated** (Tsofen, Israel) · **€3.6M raised** (Citruma, Morocco). `The evidence →`
